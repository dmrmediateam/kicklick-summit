Privacy Policy

KickStart Summit 2026 · KickLick, LLC · 627 Jackson St, Wausau, WI 54403 · Effective August 1, 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how KickLick, LLC ("KL," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information in connection with KickStart Summit 2026 (the "Event"), the Event website, and Event registration. By purchasing a ticket, registering for Event updates, or attending the Event, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact & registration information: your name, email address, phone number, company or business name, and any information you provide on registration or signup forms.
  • Payment information: ticket purchases are processed through Dayframer (dayframer.com), our forms and payments platform, and its payment processor. Card numbers are handled by the payment processor; KL does not store full card numbers.
  • Event media: photographs, video, and audio recorded at the Event, which may include your image, likeness, and voice.
  • Website usage data: we use analytics tools (including Microsoft Clarity) and cookies to understand how visitors use the Event website, including pages viewed, device information, and general location. See Section 8.
  • Communications: emails, texts, or messages you exchange with us.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to: process ticket purchases and manage registration; operate and staff the Event; send Event updates, schedules, and logistics; send marketing communications about the Event and about KL's products and services; promote the Event, including through photos and video from the Event; improve the Event website; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing With Sponsors & Partners — Please Read

By registering for or attending the Event, you understand and agree that KL may share your contact information — including your name, email address, phone number, and company — with Event sponsors, exhibitors, and partners, who may use it to contact you with marketing and promotional communications about their own products and services. KL may also sell, rent, license, or otherwise transfer attendee contact information (including email addresses and phone numbers) to third parties, including for those third parties' own marketing purposes, and KL may receive compensation for doing so.

You may opt out of this sharing and sale at any time as described in Section 7. Sponsors and other recipients are required to honor opt-out requests in their own communications and to comply with applicable marketing laws.

5. Text Messages & Calls

If you check the text-message consent box at checkout or otherwise provide express consent, you agree to receive marketing and informational calls and text messages from KL, and from Event sponsors and partners with whom your number is shared under Section 4, at the phone number you provide, including messages sent using automated technology. Consent to receive marketing texts is not a condition of purchasing a ticket. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. Reply STOP to any text to opt out of that sender's messages, or contact us as described in Section 7. If you do not check the consent box, your number will be used only for transactional Event communications (such as tickets, schedule changes, and day-of logistics).

6. Other Sharing

We also share personal information with: service providers who help us run the Event and its website (including Dayframer, our forms and payments platform, and email/text delivery providers), who are permitted to use it only to provide services to us; professional advisors; a successor in the event of a sale or reorganization of KL's business, in which case this Policy's commitments follow the data; and authorities where required by law.

7. Your Choices & Opt-Outs

  • Email: every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Opt-outs are honored within 10 business days.
  • Texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Sponsor sharing & sale of your information: email tony@kicklick.com with the subject "Data Opt-Out" from the email address you registered with. We will exclude your information from future sponsor sharing and from any sale, rental, or license of attendee lists, within 10 business days. We honor such requests regardless of your state of residence.
  • Photos/video: attendance at a public event means you may appear in Event media; see the Ticket Terms & Conditions, Section 14.

8. Cookies & Analytics

The Event website uses cookies and similar technologies, including Microsoft Clarity, which captures usage data such as pages visited, clicks, scrolling, and device information to help us improve the site. You can limit cookies through your browser settings. For more about Clarity's practices, see Microsoft's privacy statement.

9. Data Retention & Security

We retain attendee information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including marketing future KL events, unless you opt out. We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect personal information; however, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Event website and registration are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The version in effect when you provide your information governs that information. If we make a material change to how we use or share previously collected personal information, we will provide notice (such as by email to registered attendees) and, where required by law, obtain consent before applying the change to previously collected information.
