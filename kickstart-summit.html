Questions

Everything else you're wondering

Who is this event for?

Business owners, entrepreneurs, side-hustlers ready to go full-time, and go-getter professionals. If you're building something — or serious about starting — you belong here. Every workshop is hands-on and built for doers, not spectators.

What's included with my ticket?

All 3 keynotes, all 3 panels, your pick of 7 workshops across 3 blocks, catered lunch, the closing Hour of Power, and facilitated networking all day. The only things that cost extra are the food trucks and the optional VIP Friday kickoff.

What if it rains?

Rain or shine. Every session space is covered — the pavilions, the tent, and the chalet — and Wisconsin weather doesn't scare us. Dress for October and bring your notebook.

What's the refund policy?

All sales are final — see our Ticket Terms & Conditions for full details. Tickets are fully transferable, though: can't make it? Send someone from your team in your place.

Can I bring my team?

Absolutely — some of the best takeaways happen when two people from the same business hit different workshop rooms and compare notes. Each person needs a ticket; bringing 5 or more? Email info@kicklick.com for group rates.

Where do I park?

Free parking in the lots off Sylvan Street, right at the Main Gate. Registration opens 7:00 AM — come early, the coffee's on.
